# Adoption — software that pulls people onto Pulse

Goal: people choose **PulseChain because our stack is the easiest / safest / only obvious path** — not because of another farm brochure.

ROI-ranked. Prefer free/cheap distribution + unique custody UX over paid ads.

---

## Why someone switches chains (software angle)

1. **Custody they trust** (Trezor without MetaMask hell)
2. **Pay / tip / scan** that just works on phone
3. **One job done** (HEX, swap, bunker) better than ETH tools
4. **Social proof** (“this is how Pulse people actually move money”)
5. **Default paths** (links, QR, dapps open *our* wallet)

Our wedge: **Tip (phone) + Desk (Trezor) + SYNC + honest Suite gap** = Pulse-native hardware story nobody else owns.
